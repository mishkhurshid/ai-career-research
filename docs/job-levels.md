# Real AI Jobs by Career Level

*Research started November 5, 2026. This is a growing evidence log, not a complete or guaranteed-current list of vacancies.*

This page compares actual employer career listings and career programs. **An internship, new-graduate role, and experienced position can involve very different responsibilities, even when the job titles sound similar.**

## Internships

### Palantir — Forward Deployed Software Engineer, Internship — Commercial

**Employer listing:** [Palantir open positions](https://www.palantir.com/careers/open-positions/)

**Locations listed:** Chicago, New York, London, and Seoul.

**Role focus:** Forward-deployed engineering works toward technical and operational outcomes for a specific customer. Palantir describes its internship program as hands-on, with mentorship and technical learning opportunities.

**Evidence to prepare:** A coding project with clear requirements, implementation, tests, and an explanation of the customer problem it solves. This is a portfolio recommendation, not a verbatim employer requirement.

**Eligibility note:** Palantir's [early talent program](https://www.palantir.com/careers/students-and-early-talent) currently says internships are for students graduating in 2028 and should be the student's final internship before graduation. Verify eligibility and the individual listing before applying.

## Mid-level and senior-level research

These labels require extra care: **a company may not call a role “mid-level” or “senior” even when it requests substantial experience**. Do not assign a level from the title alone.

## Senior-level: additional verified employer example

### Bubble — Senior Applied AI Engineer

**[Official job description](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/bubble/32a3ade2-1e62-4ad9-9ab8-32036d6f7b6b/)**

**Location:** New York City. **Published base salary:** $201,000–$261,000, plus equity (as displayed during research on November 5, 2026).

**Responsibilities:** Improve LLM reasoning and retrieval; develop and maintain production LLM pipelines; build evaluation and latency optimization processes; improve AI-assisted app-building workflows; and own AI feature quality through deployment and monitoring.

**Employer requirements:** 5+ years of industry experience in software or ML engineering, or an advanced AI/ML/NLP degree with 3+ years of production experience. The listing also requests real experience shipping LLM features, agentic workflows, RAG, retrieval, and evaluation.

**Evidence to prepare (research recommendation, not an employer-mandated portfolio):** A deployed LLM application with documented evaluations, failure analysis, retrieval testing, monitoring, and iteration history.

**Career-level lesson:** Senior-level work requires demonstrating reliable production outcomes and ownership, not simply calling an LLM API.

## Career-level comparison: what changes?

| Career level | Typical focus | Strong portfolio or professional evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internship | Learn and contribute with mentorship | Tested code, a small working project, clear technical explanation |
| Entry-level | Build features as part of a team | Deployed application, backend/API, database, documentation |
| Mid-level | Independently deliver projects | Production integrations, ownership, evaluation, troubleshooting |
| Senior | Own architecture, quality, and difficult tradeoffs | Shipped systems, measurable improvements, monitoring, leadership |

*These are generalized research interpretations, not universal requirements. Specific employers define levels differently.*

## How to evaluate evidence of ability

For each posting, separate **explicit employer requirements** from **our proposed portfolio evidence**. A job posting might require Python or deployed software experience; a GitHub project is one possible way to demonstrate those skills, not necessarily a mandatory application artifact.
